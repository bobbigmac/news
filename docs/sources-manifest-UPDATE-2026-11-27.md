# Sources manifest — UPDATE additions

**Prepared:** 27 November 2026  
**Import status:** completed 27 November 2026

All 118 source blocks originally present in this delta have been reviewed and the relevant facts imported. The source blocks have been removed so this file is not mistaken for a reusable import queue. Do not import this file again; create a new dated update file for further source research.

## What was imported

- Concise `kind`, ownership, governance, publishing-model, history and source-specific watch notes into `src/trees/profiles/sources.json`.
- Evidence links supporting those claims, with publisher self-description kept distinct from independent evidence where that distinction matters.
- Tree placements for previously unplaced or sparse sources, including Axios, BuzzFeed, The Hill, Global News, Economic Times/News18, Interfax-Ukraine, People Daily, The Conversation, Mongabay, Nautilus, Endpoints, Investing.com, Benzinga and `dev.to/wlz`.
- Explicit relation corrections: Engadget/BGR under Static Media, GameSpot under Fandom, CNET under Ziff Davis, Mashable retained under Ziff Davis rather than Static Media, Sky News UK moved out of the US Comcast branch, and `inquirer.com` kept separate from the Philippine Daily Inquirer.
- Kind corrections for `obits.oregonlive.com`, `factcheck.afp.com` and `dev.to/wlz`.
- Measurement/display corrections needed by the systems: the Philippine Daily Inquirer measurement group excludes Philadelphia, canonical display labels are centralised in `src/source-labels.js`, and the measured-source export now resolves the corrected relationship anchors.

## What was deliberately not copied

- Corpus-observation percentage lines. They are snapshot-specific detector incidence, not durable source facts.
- Repetitive warnings that a publisher policy is self-described and not independent proof. That limitation is now carried once in the profile/methodology framing rather than pasted into every entry.
- Generic instructions to check bylines, sponsorship, opinion labels or underlying documents where the update added no source-specific fact.
- Broad quality judgements, rankings or inferred editorial intent not supported by the cited evidence.
