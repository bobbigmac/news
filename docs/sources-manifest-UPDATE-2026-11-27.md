## Corpus observation note

Where included, title-only observations are calculated from the supplied 27 November 2026 headline export. The percentages are incidence of a heuristic detector among that source’s unique headlines in this snapshot. They describe headline language, not article-body verification, actual originality, factual accuracy or the value of the underlying story. Low sample sizes are stated. “Near-duplicate title proxy” means lexical similarity across feeds, not a proven syndication relationship.

### PBS
- Key: `pbs.org`
- Source: [https://pbs.org](https://pbs.org)
- ADD: What we know:
  - Kind: US public-media membership organisation, not a single centrally staffed national newsroom. PBS is a nonprofit corporation whose members are local public television stations; national programming is supplied by member stations, independent producers and PBS itself.
  - Editorial model: PBS distributes and curates programming while member stations retain local editorial and operational autonomy. Its standards address accuracy, fairness, independence, funder influence and corrections. A PBS-branded item may therefore originate with a station or an external producer rather than a unified PBS newsroom.
  - Funding/independence: PBS is funded principally through member-station assessments, distribution revenue, underwriting, grants and contributions; it is not government-run. Important current change: the Corporation for Public Broadcasting (CPB) voted to dissolve on 5 January 2026 after Congress rescinded $1.1 billion in FY2026–27 appropriations in July 2025. CPB’s wind-down removed the former central federal funding channel and its $32 million annual National Programming Service grant to PBS; PBS cut FY2026 station assessments and reduced costs. Do not describe CPB support as an ongoing funding stream. This does not mean PBS or its member stations ceased operating.
  - Watch: identify the producing station, programme or external production company for individual stories. The PBS brand is a distribution/quality framework, not proof that every item was independently reported by PBS staff.
  - ADD: Corpus observation (20 unique headlines, 2026-11-27 snapshot): 60.0% explicit event/action; 30.0% material consequence; 20.0% evidence/provenance cue; 5.0% near-duplicate title proxy. Title-only heuristic incidence; not an article-quality or factuality verdict.

- ADD: What we know:

## Residual limitations

- This update adds researched profiles and corrections for 101 source entries, prioritising meaningful corpus presence, material institutional distinctions, and documented ownership/editorial changes. It does not pretend that every one-off domain in the 695-source manifest has enough public evidence for a meaningful dossier.
- Individual Substack/DEV/Reddit/GitHub/Medium/social-platform feeds are not publishers in the same sense as staffed newsrooms. Their author or account should be assessed separately from the hosting platform.
- For sources with fewer than 10 headlines in the supplied export, no corpus-based practice claim is made unless explicitly stated. Sparse title samples cannot establish a stable editorial pattern.
- Ownership, funding and policies are time-sensitive. The update reflects public information located as of 27 November 2026; where public disclosure was insufficient, the file says so rather than inferring control.
- All title-derived observations are descriptive flags from the prior heuristic tooling. They are not validated article-quality scores and should not be used as source-level factuality claims.
